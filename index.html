Company Overview

Advance Pharma Technology is an MDI (Metered Dose Inhaler) production facility currently under construction, designed to specialize in the manufacturing of high-quality MDI pharmaceutical products. Our facility is being developed to fully comply with GCC and EU GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) regulations, ensuring the highest standards of quality, safety, and regulatory alignment.

With a strong commitment to excellence, Advance Pharma Technology aims to serve regional and international markets by delivering reliable and compliant MDI solutions to support respiratory healthcare needs. Our focus is on advanced production capabilities, cutting-edge technology, and strict adherence to global pharmaceutical standards — laying the foundation for a trusted name in MDI manufacturing with annual production rate of 4 million units/year.
